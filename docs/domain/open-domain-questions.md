# Open Domain Questions

These decisions are deliberately deferred:

- Whether Customer and Supplier eventually need a shared Party/Contact abstraction.
- Inventory valuation method and cost-of-goods-sold timing.
- Organization/product negative-stock policy.
- Branch-specific price books and document-numbering behavior.
- Tax model depth and verified statutory rules/rates.
- Accounting posting timing and chart-of-accounts structure.
- Payment, banking, tax, barcode, and receipt-printer provider choices.
- Retention/archival periods and detailed fiscal-year configuration.

These are future ADR or sprint-specific design candidates, not blockers for the platform foundation.
